01 Readings

How do I scan my monitor?

Tap “Scan monitor” and point the frame at your monitor's display. Hold the phone still for a second or two: once several frames in a row show the same numbers, the values lock in and the phone gives a short vibration. Then check the numbers and save the reading.

The numbers aren't recognized. What can I do?

Hold the phone straight in front of the display, 15–30 cm away, without glare from a lamp or window. In the dark, turn on the light with the button at the top. You can also choose a photo from your library or type the numbers. If your monitor isn't recognized at all, send us a photo of it with the model name — it helps improve recognition (only if you want to).

How does voice entry work?

Tap the wave button and say, for example, “one thirty over eighty-five, pulse seventy-two”. English, German, French and other languages with an on-device model are recognized on the iPhone; Ukrainian and Russian are recognized by Apple's speech service. Voice entry is part of Pro.

What do the colors and categories mean?

Colors show reference ranges for office blood pressure from the 2023 ESC/ESH guidelines. They are not a diagnosis: home readings are usually lower, and interpretation is up to your doctor.

02 Data

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. The app has no server and does not send your readings to us or anyone else. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Your data moves with your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) or a direct transfer to the new phone. You can also keep a separate copy: Profile → Export CSV.

How do I delete data?

A single reading: open it in the journal and tap “Delete reading”. A person with all their readings: Profile → Edit → “Delete person”. Deleting the app removes all its data from the device.

How do I send a report to my doctor?

Trends → “Doctor report”: pick a period and tap Share — send the PDF by messenger or email, or print it. The report is part of Pro.

03 Purchases

What does Pro include?

Voice entry and the PDF doctor report. Pro is a one-time purchase, no subscription. Manual entry, monitor scanning, charts and medication tracking are free.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID and the purchase restores automatically. If it doesn't, open the Pro screen and tap “Restore purchases”.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

04 Other

Is this a medical device?

No. Tensio is a journal of readings. It does not measure blood pressure, diagnose or recommend treatment. Discuss your readings with a doctor.

Which iPhones are supported?

iOS 26 or later.
