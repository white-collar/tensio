Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

  • Your readings, profiles, medication and notes are stored only on your iPhone. We never receive them.
  • No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Photos of your monitor are read on the device and are not stored.
  • For languages without an on-device model (such as Ukrainian), voice entry is recognized by Apple's speech service — not by us.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.

01 Who we are

Tensio (“Сангвін” in Ukrainian) is developed by Eugene Shmorgun, an independent developer based in Ukraine (“we”). For privacy questions, write to eugen.shmorgun@gmail.com.

02 What data the app holds and where

The app stores the data you enter yourself: blood pressure and pulse readings, the irregular heartbeat indicator, reading times, people's profiles (name, year of birth, monitor model), medication and intakes, and notes.

All of it is kept in the app's storage on your device. The app has no server, creates no accounts and does not send your data to us or to anyone else. This is health data, so the app is deliberately built for it never to leave your phone.

03 Camera and photos

To read the numbers on your monitor's display, the app uses the camera or a photo you choose. A model built into the app does the recognition on the device. Camera frames are processed in memory and not stored; library photos are neither copied nor uploaded.

04 Voice entry

For languages for which Apple provides an on-device speech model (for example English, German, French, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese), audio is processed only on your iPhone.

For Ukrainian, Russian, Polish and other languages without such a model yet, iOS sends the audio to Apple's speech recognition service. We receive neither the audio nor the text. Apple processes it under its privacy policy, and iOS says so when it asks for speech recognition permission. The app shows where your voice is recognized. If you prefer not to use Apple's service, simply don't use voice entry — everything else works without it.

05 Purchases

The Pro purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details, name or email address. The app only checks that the purchase exists, as signed by Apple. From Apple we receive aggregated sales reports only.

06 What we don't do

We use no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs; we do not sell or share data, and we do not profile users.

07 Crash reports

If “Share With App Developers” is on in iOS settings, Apple may share anonymized crash and diagnostic reports with us. Apple collects and anonymizes them; they do not contain your readings. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

08 Backups and export

The app's data is included in your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) if you make one; backups are handled by Apple according to your settings. CSV and PDF files you export go wherever you send them, under your control and that of the service you choose.

09 Retention and deletion

Data is kept until you delete it. You can delete individual readings, medication and profiles in the app; deleting the app removes all of its data from the device. We hold no copies, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

10 Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Ukraine's Law “On Personal Data Protection”, you have the right to access, rectify, erase and port your data. Because the data exists only on your device, you exercise these rights directly in the app (view, edit, delete, export to CSV). Contact us with any question. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: in Ukraine, the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights; in the EU, the data protection authority of your country.

11 Children

The app is not intended for children under 16.

12 Medical disclaimer

Tensio is a journal of readings. It is not a medical device, does not measure blood pressure, and does not diagnose or recommend treatment.

13 Changes

If we change this policy, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date. We will mention material changes in the app's update notes.
